# EX GAMES LIVING MAP — LAUNCH BASELINE 001

## Purpose

Define when Living Map geography is sufficiently reliable for launch without requiring exhaustive geographic completeness.

## Accepted unknowns

The following are acceptable at launch:

- a locality with zero discovered participants;
- a participant explicitly retained at region-only resolution;
- an unregistered minor landscape, reserve, forest, wetland, bay, catchment or other long-tail geographic feature;
- incomplete enrichment of otherwise functioning geography;
- authoritative landscape geometry that has not yet been acquired where the basemap still provides truthful geographic context.

These are backlog/enrichment states, not defects.

## Not acceptable at launch

The following require correction or an explicit launch decision:

- a participant silently lost from the map;
- a participant assigned to geography that does not exist in the LM without being explicitly marked unresolved;
- invented or knowingly false geographic precision;
- locality/region selection that fails;
- participant profiles that fail to load;
- geometry that materially breaks map rendering or interaction;
- a conspicuous geographic omission that makes the participant/ecological story materially misleading.

## Stop rule

Geographic refinement stops for launch when:

1. participant and profile functionality is reliable;
2. all participants are either geographically resolved or explicitly unresolved/region-only;
3. remaining locality gaps predominantly represent zero discovered participants rather than broken data;
4. major conspicuous landscape omissions identified during browser review have either been fixed or consciously accepted;
5. additional work is predominantly long-tail enrichment rather than correction of material defects.

## Post-launch principle

The Living Map is expected to improve continuously. New participant discovery, participant corrections, authoritative datasets and Area Model enrichment can expand coverage without redefining the launch baseline.
